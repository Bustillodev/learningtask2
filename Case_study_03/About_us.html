ABOUT US

Our History

The University of St. La Salle (USLS) is a Catholic private research university run by the De La Salle Brothers. Located along La Salle Avenue in Bacolod City, Negros Occidental, Philippines, it was established in 1952 as La Salle College - Bacolod, making it the second oldest campus founded by the congregation in the country.

What started with only 175 male students under seven faculty members has grown exponentially over the decades. The institution expanded its offerings to include higher education and officially became co-educational in 1966. On July 5, 1988, the college was formally elevated to university status, continuing its legacy of excellence in education.

Academic Excellence & Community

Today, USLS stands as a premier educational institution in the region, known for its diverse colleges—spanning Business, Law, Medicine, Engineering, Nursing, Education, and Arts and Sciences. Grounded in the Lasallian tradition, the university prepares students to become globally competent professionals who are dedicated to social transformation and community service.

Core Values

  • Spirit of Faith: Discerning God's active presence in human history.
  • Zeal for Service: The passionate commitment to actively participate in the collective transformation of society.
  • Communion in Mission: Working together as a cohesive community in solidarity with the poor.

MISSION & VISION

Institutional Vision

The University of St. La Salle envisions itself as a premier learner-centered Christian institution, recognized for excellence in teaching, research, and community engagement, working together for social transformation and sustainable development.

Institutional Mission Statement

True to our Catholic tradition and inspired by St. John Baptist de La Salle, the University of St. La Salle is a learning community that forms persons of integrity and excellence, committed to the shared mission of caring for peoples and the Earth.

Core Declaration

Deeply moved by the plight of the poor and youth at risk, we commit ourselves to providing a human and Christian education. Guided by the Lasallian spirit of faith and zeal, we aim to do everything for God and attribute all to God, enabling individuals to live a life of dignity to the fullest of their capabilities.

University of Saint La Salle (USLS) Bacolod City, Philippines is a Lasallian Catholic university committed to providing excellent, value-laden education that forms globally competent, socially responsible, and faith-inspired graduates.

Rooted in the Lasallian tradition, USLS provides a transformative education that goes beyond academics, shaping the whole person in mind, heart, and spirit.

Lasallian Values

Rooted in the charism of St. John Baptist de La Salle—faith, zeal for service, and communion in mission guide every aspect of university life.

ABOUT US

Our Story

Founded in Negros Occidental, Negros Information Technology Solutions (NITS) began with a clear mission: to bridge the digital gap for local enterprises. What started as a small group of passionate tech professionals providing on-the-ground hardware support has grown into a reliable, full-service IT solutions agency serving businesses across the province.

As industries shift rapidly into the online landscape, NITS remains committed to delivering accessible, fast, and secure digital architectures. We take pride in being a homegrown firm that understands the exact unique needs, goals, and opportunities of businesses within our local communities.

Why Choose NITS?

We combine technical expertise with true local accessibility. Unlike foreign agencies, we are physically present in the region to deploy, monitor, and troubleshoot systems instantly—ensuring your operations face minimal downtime.

Our Core Pillars

  • Local Innovation: Engineering customized, practical technologies that cater directly to Negrense workflows.
  • Reliability: Providing steadfast network infrastructures and active data backups you can lean on.
  • Community Empowerment: Uplifting homegrown businesses by making modern enterprise tools affordable and intuitive.

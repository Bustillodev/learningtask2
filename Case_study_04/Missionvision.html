MISSION & VISION

Our Vision

To be the leading and most trusted homegrown IT solutions provider in Negros Occidental, recognized for driving digital transformation, fostering local tech talent, and empowering community businesses to thrive in a global economy.

Our Mission

At Negros Information Technology Solutions (NITS), our mission is to deliver dependable, secure, and accessible technology frameworks that streamline operations for local enterprises. We commit ourselves to being physically present and culturally aligned with our clients, providing swift on-site support and customized software engineering that directly addresses local challenges.

Our Core Intent

We aim to dismantle the technical barriers that prevent micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in our province from scaling. By offering affordable enterprise-grade tools, robust network hosting, and continuous tech education, NITS dedicates its efforts to building a highly competitive, fully connected, and innovative Negrense digital ecosystem.

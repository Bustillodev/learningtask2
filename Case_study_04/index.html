WELCOME TO NITS!!!

Negros Information Technology Solutions (NITS) is a premier, homegrown IT service provider dedicated to empowering businesses, schools, and local enterprises across Negros Occidental through world-class digital innovation.

Driving Digital Growth in Negros

Headquartered right in the heart of the region, we specialize in building custom software applications, networking setups, and localized IT support systems. Our services are engineered specifically to modernize workflows and improve the digital landscape of businesses across the province.

Empowering Local Enterprises

From helping local micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) transition into e-commerce to setting up secure corporate databases, the NITS team of dedicated tech professionals designs reliable, accessible, and fast tech frameworks tailored for our community.

Local Innovation & Technology Ecosystem

We believe in nurturing local tech talents and providing immediate, on-the-ground technical solutions. Stay up to date with our newest systems, IT deployment projects, and digital solutions built to elevate the Negrense community.

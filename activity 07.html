Ingredients

  • Dried Rice Noodles (Bihon): The base of the dish.
  • Proteins: A combination of chicken, pork, Chinese sausage, and shrimp
  • Aromatics: Minced garlic and chopped onions.
  • Cabbage: Shredded.
  • Carrots: Cut into matchsticks.
  • Celery: Cut into matchsticks.
  • Snap Peas: Trimmed for stir-frying.
  • Chicken Broth: The primary cooking liquid for the noodles.
  • Soy Sauce: For savory flavor and color.
  • Oyster Sauce: For added umami and sweetness.
  • Cooking Oil: For sautéing.
  • Salt & Pepper: To adjust final seasoning.
  • Green Onions: Sliced thin.
  • Fried Garlic Bits: For crunch.
  • Calamansi or Lemon Wedges: To be squeezed over the dish before eating.

Steps

  1. Soak the dried rice noodles in a bowl of warm water for about 5 minutes until they soften, then drain them completely.
  2. Slice your chicken, pork, or Chinese sausage into thin strips and prepare your shrimp by peeling and deveining them.
  3. Chop your vegetables by shredding the cabbage and cutting the carrots and celery into thin, matchstick-sized pieces.
  4. Heat oil in a large wok or pan over medium-high heat and sauté the minced garlic and chopped onions until they are fragrant and translucent.
  5. Add the meat to the pan and sear it until it is fully cooked and slightly browned on the edges.
  6. Toss in the carrots, celery, and snap peas, stir-frying them for a few minutes until they are tender yet still crisp.
  7. Pour in the chicken broth, soy sauce, and oyster sauce, then bring the entire mixture to a boil.
  8. Add the softened rice noodles into the boiling liquid and toss them continuously until they absorb all the flavorful broth.
  9. Mix the shredded cabbage and the cooked shrimp back into the pan, stirring for another minute until the cabbage wilts slightly.
  10. Season the dish with salt and pepper to taste before transferring it to a serving plate.
  11. Garnish the top with sliced green onions and fried garlic bits for extra flavor and crunch.
  12. Serve the dish immediately with fresh calamansi or lemon wedges to squeeze over the noodles right before eating.
